# x86QW 0.6.0

A functional release of the background service lifecycle and of a full review
of the installer menus.

## Services

- starts MVDSV, QTV and QWFWD in the foreground or in the background;
- adds a central view of the running stack, its endpoints and its public
  parameters;
- requests a coordinated shutdown from the menu or with `status --stop`;
- keeps passwords out of the command line, the journal and the status output;
- checks the lock, the controller's identity and the journal before stopping
  processes;
- keeps private logs of the background session and removes the lock when it
  finishes.

## Interface

- shows `x86qw.cmd` in the equivalent Windows commands and `./x86qw.sh` on
  macOS/Linux;
- avoids cutting off the plan and the result of `update` on narrow terminals;
- shows the settings, a summary and a safe command before playing or hosting;
- keeps numbered options, aligned descriptions and consistent navigation
  between parent menus;
- includes the actions to view and stop services in the Services group.

## KTX and gameplay

- keeps the 24 declared modes and the Frogbot roster according to each
  usermode's rules;
- keeps the skins, colors and teams KTX controls;
- keeps the contextual key plans and quitting directly with F12 in the five
  current games;
- adds no new runtime, game, mod or map.

## Validation

- 362 maintenance tests and four site tests;
- a real smoke of QTV and QWFWD in the background on macOS, including the
  query and the coordinated shutdown;
- the Ubuntu, macOS and Windows CI matrix with Python 3.10 and 3.13;
- catalog, Git LFS, zipapp, immutable bundle and site validated before
  publishing.
