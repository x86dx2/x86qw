# x86QW 1.0.1

The source and the current installer move to `1.0.1`. `0.7.13` stays in the
catalog as the historical baseline, not as current.

## What goes in

- a fix of the dedicated `host` on MVDSV 1.11 (a short ephemeral cfg, hex RCON,
  no autoexec of the session's `server.cfg`, `sv_crypt_rcon` turned back on for
  an external bind);
- `status --json`, `doctor` and `ui` accept the `--target` the launcher
  injects;
- non-KTX games without `--mode` no longer cancel on EOF;
- capabilities already in the tree (`doctor`, profiles, library, presets, the
  local UI) become 1.0.x; there is no 1.1 line at this stage.

## What does not change

- the `owner-only` audience; `external-public` stays `NO-GO`;
- the `0.7.13` and `1.0.0` packages and tags stay immutable;
- the `0.7.0–0.7.13` migration fixtures stay as history, not as current;
- production TUF and the native M3 evidence of the exact candidate stay gates
  separate from the public promotion.

## Validation

- tests of `host_spec`, the launcher's `--target` and non-KTX games;
- `manage.py verify` and the portable suite after the source cut.
