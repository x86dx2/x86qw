# x86QW 1.0.2

The source and the current installer move to `1.0.2`. `1.0.1` and `0.7.13`
stay in the catalog as historical, not as current.

## What goes in

- `migrate --dry-run` authenticates the `1.0.x` line (including
  `1.0.1`/`1.0.2`); a current installation is no longer treated as history
  without a family;
- `cleanup` finishes the Darwin cache when TUF's `root.json` is a symlink.

## What does not change

- the `owner-only` audience; `external-public` stays `NO-GO`;
- the `0.7.13`, `1.0.0` and `1.0.1` packages and tags stay immutable;
- the `0.7.0–0.7.13` migration fixtures stay as history, not as current.

## Validation

- state/`migrate` tests for `1.0.1` and `cleanup` tests with a TUF symlink;
- the native M3 harness 25/25 on the exact digest of this zip.
