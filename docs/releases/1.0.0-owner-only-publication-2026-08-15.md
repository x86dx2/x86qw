The public lifecycle proved `version`, `changes`, `update --dry-run`,
`update --yes`, a second idempotent update, `verify`, a conservative uninstall
and `uninstall --purge`. The `0.7.13` migration was not run in this
acceptance, because the scope is `single-user` and the project has not been
opened to external users yet. The protected M3 harness already keeps the
migration capability for the future `external-public` transition.

## Decision

`1.0.0 owner-only`: **GO**. The next stage is the maintainer's own use and
operational observation. Historical migration, an external soak, independent
TUF custody, Gatekeeper/notarization of the original upstream bundle and the
promotion of other platforms stay conditional on a future declaration of
`release_audience=external-public` or on a later specific decision.
