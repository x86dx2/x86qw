# x86QW 0.2.1

A corrective release of the lifecycle and the maintenance surfaces of the
platform consolidated in `0.2.0`.

## Fixes

- an atomic lock of one stack per installation, taken before recovery;
- a controller and child identity that resists PID reuse;
- conservative shutdown of confirmed orphans, keeping a divergent or
  inconclusive identity untouched;
- mandatory logical removal of the sensitive ephemeral configurations;
- a QTV upstream consistent with IPv4/IPv6 binds and a separate warning for
  exposed HTTP;
- `repair` recognized by the launchers, with unlimited arguments on Windows;
- repair of ezQuake stable/nightly, permissions, macOS preparation and partial
  metadata, without downgrades or changes to personal settings;
- the whitespace gate applied to the range actually committed in the PR/push;
- install capabilities limited to the explicitly enabled set;
- `compatibility.json` as the source of truth for the relations between
  runtime, game, service and protocol.

## Compatibility and limits

The commands, profiles, five games, stable/nightly and state formats 1 and 2
are kept. There is no new runtime, engine, game, mod or map. Graphical smokes
and native network smokes that were already manual still need separate
validation. Version `0.2.0` stays immutable in the history.
