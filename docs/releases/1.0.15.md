# x86QW 1.0.15

The source and the current installer move to `1.0.15`. `1.0.14` stays
historical and immutable on the mirrors.

## What goes in

- centers the logo on the terminal's real width in PowerShell, Bash and
  Python;
- keeps the alignment right even when `COLUMNS` holds an inherited value;
- brings titles, questions, selection, search, progress, success and error
  into the same design system;
- keeps the compact plan and the transparency of the install flow.

## What does not change

- the `owner-only` audience; `external-public` stays `NO-GO`;
- earlier versions stay historical and immutable;
- SHA-256, size, ZIP and TUF metadata stay fail-closed;
- Windows and Linux stay preview; the operational gate stays Apple M3.

## Validation

- regression tests run the real bootstraps in a PTY with a geometry and
  `COLUMNS` that disagree;
- the full maintenance suite and the portable contracts;
- the canonical and public projections of the bootstraps are compared byte for
  byte;
- the public promotion is verified after publishing.
