# x86QW 1.0.3

The source and the current installer move to `1.0.3`. `1.0.2`, `1.0.1`,
`1.0.0` and `0.7.13` stay in the catalog as historical, not as current.

## What goes in

- an interactive TUI with a clearer hierarchy, Match/Installation groups,
  visible search, selection that does not depend on color, and
  Home/End/PageUp/PageDown;
- the menu asks "O que você quer fazer?" ("What do you want to do?") instead of
  showing a slogan.

## What does not change

- the `owner-only` audience; `external-public` stays `NO-GO`;
- the `0.7.13`, `1.0.0`, `1.0.1` and `1.0.2` packages and tags stay immutable;
- the `0.7.0–0.7.13` migration fixtures stay as history, not as current.

## Validation

- tests of the menu engine, the installed menu's contracts and the play/KTX
  alignment;
- the portable Validate at the source cut.
