# x86QW 1.0.9

The source and the current installer move to `1.0.9`. `1.0.8` stays
historical and immutable on the mirrors.

## What goes in

- adopts a guided three-stage terminal flow inspired by the OpenClaw installer
  analyzed in a sandbox;
- applies the same palette, typographic hierarchy, status symbols and
  progress rhythm to the Unix and PowerShell bootstraps and to the interactive
  x86QW menu;
- presents the plan, the install and the finish with short, predictable
  choices;
- keeps output free of ANSI when the terminal does not take colors or is
  redirected;
- keeps the persistent success and error messages readable outside a TTY.

## What does not change

- the `owner-only` audience; `external-public` stays `NO-GO`;
- earlier versions stay historical and immutable;
- SHA-256, size, ZIP and TUF metadata stay fail-closed;
- Windows and Linux stay preview; the operational gate stays Apple M3.

## Validation

- 1,985 full maintenance tests, with 39 expected skips;
- 16 site tests;
- portable contracts on macOS and Ubuntu with Python 3.10 and 3.13;
- CodeQL with no new alerts;
- an isolated TTY smoke on Linux;
- deterministic validation of the bundle, catalog, mirrors and TUF.
