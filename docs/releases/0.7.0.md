# x86QW 0.7.0

A consistency release of the KTX modes and the Frogbot integration.

## Modes and maps

- offers only maps that have the assets the selected mode needs: a Frogbot
  route, a Race route or a CTF entity;
- keeps `3on3` as two teams of three players and `2on2on2` as three teams of
  two;
- keeps `3v3` and `2v2v2` as command-line aliases;
- checks usermodes, entry settings, help commands and binds against the real
  KTX 1.47 content.

## Frogbots

- fills fixed rosters according to each usermode's number of players and
  teams;
- applies a random skill to each bot independently;
- keeps the KTX, random x86QW and personal list name profiles;
- fixes repeated identities between teammates and opponents;
- removes the old experimental appearance cvars from the QVM: skins and colors
  follow KTX's behavior entirely again.

## Integrity

- rebuilds the KTX 1.47 QVM reproducibly and records sources, patches, symbols
  and hashes;
- strengthens the declarative verifier to catch inconsistent maps, assets,
  usermodes, settings or commands before publishing;
- adds no new runtime, game, mod or map.

## Validation

- 373 maintenance tests and four site tests;
- a deterministic build of the KTX component;
- a windowed smoke on macOS with Frogbot fill, random skill, several teams and
  quitting with F12;
- the Ubuntu, macOS and Windows CI matrix with Python 3.10 and 3.13 required
  before publishing.
