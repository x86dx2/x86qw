# x86QW 1.0.12

The source and the current installer move to `1.0.12`. `1.0.11` stays
historical and immutable on the mirrors.

## What goes in

- names each component by name, version and size during the install;
- shows the exact position of each component in the overall progress;
- says how many files each package will have verified;
- describes, in plain language, each step of the final verification;
- keeps the results visible in the terminal's history.

## What does not change

- the `owner-only` audience; `external-public` stays `NO-GO`;
- earlier versions stay historical and immutable;
- SHA-256, size, ZIP and TUF metadata stay fail-closed;
- Windows and Linux stay preview; the operational gate stays Apple M3.

## Validation

- regression tests cover the install, verification and presentation of the
  packages;
- the full maintenance suite and the portable contracts;
- deterministic validation of the bundle, catalog, mirrors and TUF;
- the public promotion verified after publishing.
